How the score works

Supported keyword coverage: 40%. Required qualifications: 35%. Relevant experience: 25%. Keyword mentions that are negated or aspirational earn no credit. Only fully met items backed by an exact quote from your original resume earn credit; partial and unconfirmed items earn zero. Categories with no job requirements are excluded and the remaining weights are rescaled. Tailoring doesn’t change your qualifications, so we don’t inflate a second score.
